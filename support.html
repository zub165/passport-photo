Frequently Asked Questions

Does the app upload my photos?
No. All processing runs on your device. Nothing is sent to our servers.

Which paper sizes are supported?
4×6 (landscape & portrait), 5×7, 8×10, and A4.

Background replacement not working?
Works best with a solid, plain background behind the subject. Busy backgrounds may not separate cleanly. This is a creative editing tool — you are responsible for checking whether an edited photo meets the rules of whoever you submit it to.

Are the size templates official?
No. They are general references we provide for convenience and may be outdated or incorrect. Requirements vary by authority and change over time, so always confirm the current specification with the official source before printing or submitting.

Where can I print?
Any photo lab or store that prints your selected paper size. Use cutting guides to trim each photo.
